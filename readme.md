A weather app for choosing music based on your mood and the weather

The background images always match the weather currently outside

The music is based on your mood, and on the current weather

## v2 (web)

A rebuilt web version lives in [`web/`](web/). It keeps the weather-by-mood idea and replaces the bundled MP3s with ten original loops synthesized in the browser and released under CC0. See [`web/README.md`](web/README.md).
