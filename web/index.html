The collection

Ten loops, one for every sky and mood. Each is composed and synthesized by this page's own code. No samples, no outside recordings, so you can use them anywhere.

Released under CC0 1.0. Use the loops in apps, videos, streams and games, commercially or not, with no credit required. Each download includes a LICENSE.txt.

How it's made

A seeded composer picks a mode, chord progression and motif per track, then a Web Audio synth renders pads, bass, bells, plucks, keys, drums and weather sound. Same seed, same song, every time.

From v1

The 2019 iOS app paired five weather states with happy and sad moods. v2 keeps that matrix and replaces the bundled MP3s with music you own outright.
